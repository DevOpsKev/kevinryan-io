# Client Engagement Skill

## Overview
Supports business development activities including proposal creation, client communication, and technical presentations. Leverages knowledge of Kevin Ryan & Associates' client portfolio and service offerings.

## Triggers
- Proposal or pitch requests
- Client-specific questions
- Business development activities
- Technical presentation needs
- Discovery session planning

## Process

### 1. Client Context Analysis
- Review known client portfolio (CERN, Nestlé, NatWest, BBC, Financial Times, etc.)
- Understand client's industry and technical challenges
- Identify relevant case studies and experience
- Map services to client needs

### 2. Proposal Development
- Structure compelling technical proposals
- Highlight relevant platform capabilities
- Include architecture recommendations
- Provide implementation timelines

### 3. Technical Presentations
- Create clear technical narratives
- Demonstrate platform capabilities
- Address specific client requirements
- Present ROI and business value

## Tools
- `read_github_file` - Access client case studies and references
- `web_search` - Research client background and industry trends
- `create_github_file` - Generate proposals and presentations
- `list_github_directory` - Browse relevant documentation

## Outputs
- Technical proposals and RFP responses
- Client presentation materials
- Discovery session agendas
- Architecture recommendations
- Implementation roadmaps

## Examples

### Proposal Creation
For new client engagement:
1. Research client background and needs
2. Map Kevin Ryan & Associates capabilities
3. Structure technical approach
4. Include relevant case studies
5. Provide clear deliverables and timeline

### Technical Presentation
For client demos:
1. Understand audience technical level
2. Prepare platform demonstration
3. Show relevant use cases
4. Address specific questions and concerns
5. Present next steps and engagement model

### Discovery Session Planning
For initial client meetings:
1. Prepare discovery questions
2. Research client's current state
3. Identify potential opportunities
4. Plan technical deep-dive areas
5. Structure follow-up activities
