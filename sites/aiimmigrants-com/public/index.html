01The declaration

The fears we project onto AI are borrowed fears.

The parallelJob theft, cultural erosion, loss of control. Every charge levelled at the algorithm was levelled at the immigrant first. The parallels aren't coincidental.

The distractionWhat if the algorithm is the latest way to avoid harder questions — about corporate power, labour rights, and democratic accountability?

The stakesFrom deep-fake politics to data colonialism, from algorithmic landlords to digital day-labourers. Part social commentary, part survival guide.

Thirty years in technology — long enough to have seen every wave of disruption sold as revolution, and every revolution dismissed as hype.

He has led enterprise AI adoption programmes, put coding agents into the hands of engineering teams, and delivered board-level recommendations on what to do next. He knows how the machines work. He also knows who gets to decide how they're used — and who doesn't get asked.

AI Immigrants is his first book. Spec Driven Development, a methodology for engineering with AI agents, is his second. Both sides of the same question: what these systems can do, and what we should let them.
